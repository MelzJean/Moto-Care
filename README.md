# Moto-Care

MotoCare – Vehicle Maintenance Tracker

Track maintenance, service logs & reminders for your motorcycle or car.

Full Description:

Keep your motorcycle and car in peak condition with MotoCare — the all-in-one vehicle maintenance tracker designed for Filipino riders and drivers. Whether you own a motorcycle, a car, or both, MotoCare helps you stay on top of every oil change, tire rotation, brake inspection, and service appointment.

Key Features:

Vehicle Management – Add unlimited motorcycles and cars with photos, license plates, make, model, year, and odometer readings
Service Logs – Record every service visit with date, odometer, service type (Oil Change, Inspection, Tire, Brake, Other), cost, and notes
Smart Maintenance Reminders – Automatic reminders for Oil Change (5,000 km), Tire Rotation (10,000 km), and Brake Inspection (20,000 km) when you add a vehicle. Create custom reminders too
Visual Status Tracking – Color-coded badges (OK ✓, Due Soon ⚠️, Overdue 🚨) show maintenance health at a glance with remaining kilometers
Expense Tracking – Log service costs and view total spending per vehicle and across your entire garage in Philippine Pesos (₱)
Dashboard Overview – One-screen view of all alerts, vehicle cards, expense summary, and recent service history
PDF & Text Reports – Generate professional PDF service reports natively — no internet or third-party libraries needed
Offline-First – All data is stored locally on your device. No account, no internet, no data sharing

Perfect for:

Motorcycle owners who want to track "kilometros since last change"
Car owners managing multiple vehicles
Riders and drivers who prefer simple, offline tools
Anyone who wants to avoid costly repairs by staying on schedule

MotoCare is built with a modern, clean interface using Material 3 design. Works entirely offline — your data stays on your phone.
